What this can't tell you

  • It isn't an AI detector. Nothing in this report says whether an image was or wasn't made with AI.
  • A determined forger can redraw an existing image stroke by stroke (by hand, with a plotter, or with software that imitates pen input). The recording would look like ordinary drawing.
  • Tracing is checked only against the reference image given to this report, and only for line work that follows its outlines. Tracing done mostly with fills or hatching can score below the limit.
  • Pastes are found from Krita's paste and import commands, and from new layers that arrive already holding an image. Recordings made before 15 November 2026 have only the second, so they miss a paste into an existing layer and give a pasted image's size as it arrived, not after it was resized.
  • A pasted image's final size is estimated from how much its layer's outline grew or shrank. If the whole layer is resized, everything on it counts; if only part of it is, the estimate is off.
  • The checkpoints that let this report detect edits to the recording are kept with the recording, not with an independent timestamp service. Someone with access to it could rebuild the whole recording.
  • The limits used here come from a handful of test sessions by one person on one tablet, not from many illustrators.
